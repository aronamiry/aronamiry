# Behnia Amirimanesh — Founder & Creative Director of Amiry Design (London, UK)

**Behnia Amirimanesh** (born June 9, 2006 in Torbat-e Heydarieh, Iran; based in **London, United Kingdom**) is a web designer, creative director, full-stack interface engineer, and the founder and owner of **[Amiry Design](https://amirydesign.com)** (`Amiry Design Studio`), a London-headquartered web design and digital engineering studio.

Combining a formal academic background in **Social Relations** and university studies in **Finance** with custom software architecture (`React`, `Next.js`, `TypeScript`, `Tailwind CSS`, `Framer Motion`, `Node.js`), Behnia scaled Amiry Design within its opening 90 days to architect and engineer the official websites and web applications for international sports governing bodies and European organizations—including the **International Mixed Martial Arts Sport Federation (IMMASF)** and the **International Self-Defence Federation (ISDF)**.

## Selected Production Platforms Engineered by Amiry Design

1. **[International Mixed Martial Arts Sport Federation (IMMASF)](https://immasf.com)** — Four-language (`EN`, `IT`, `FR`, `DE`) international governing body portal covering global membership, national federation affiliation, governance, and event sanctioning ([Case Study](https://amirydesign.com/work/immasf)).
2. **[International Self-Defence Federation (ISDF)](https://isdf.ch)** — Official Swiss-headquartered international federation platform and real-time public instructor/coach credential verification registry at [`isdf.ch/verify`](https://isdf.ch/verify).
3. **[Equal Armor Academy (Lugano, Switzerland)](https://equalarmor.ch)** — Multilingual combat sports and self-defence academy platform engineered around streamlined trial class conversion ([Case Study](https://amirydesign.com/work/equal-armor)).
4. **[PM Media](https://pmmedia.online)** — Bilingual (`EN` / `TR`) commercial film and editorial production studio showcase ([Case Study](https://amirydesign.com/work/pm-media)).
5. **[Swiss Art Land (Zurich & Lugano)](https://amirydesign.com/work/swiss-art-land)** — Four-language art academy and exhibition platform.
